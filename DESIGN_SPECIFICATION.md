# Portfolio design specification

Michael P. Gibb, Ph.D. — Analytics and AI leadership for growth and better business decisions.

Audience: executives and hiring teams evaluating analytics, statistics, AI/ML and decision-science leadership. Preserve the established navy #0A192F, white, slate #334155, decorative copper #B87333, readable copper #925322 and light copper #E3AC79. Use readable system sans-serif, Georgia reading text and system monospace.

The homepage curates four to six evaluated studies. The research catalog prioritizes published findings and offers search plus industry, method, decision and status filters. Planned topics stay in a research agenda. Dataset selection positions are metadata, not measured performance rankings.

Completed program studies follow Decision → Implication → Evidence → Data → Method → Code. Their first screen explains the decision and finding, status, coverage and source. Executive summaries use plain language. Counts, baselines, primary metrics, uncertainty and consequential limitations remain visible. Supplementary derivations and diagnostics may expand on demand.

Charts have text equivalents, visible focus, native keyboard controls and responsive layouts. Decision controls use evaluated artifacts or clearly labeled scenario assumptions, with units, supported ranges and reset. They never imply retraining on page load. Synthetic demonstrations retain their explicit labels.
