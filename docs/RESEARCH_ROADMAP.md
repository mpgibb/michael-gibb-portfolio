# Research program

The catalog accounts for 60 studies across 20 industries. S28, S02 and S04 have executed public-data evaluations. The remaining 57 designs have no completed findings. Their source entries describe the research question, proposed evaluation, data source and material boundaries.

The initial execution sequence is S28 contact prioritization, S02 inventory planning, S04 advertising incrementality, S58 workflow delay, S60 agent reliability and S43 Chicago-area property valuation. The sequence does not reduce the program's scope. Publication follows inspected data, frozen evaluation design, executed comparisons, uncertainty, diagnostics, report, reproducible artifacts and browser verification.

The four existing commercial demonstrations remain available as explicitly synthetic evaluations. They do not count as real-data evidence for any numbered study. Independent technical review remains pending.
