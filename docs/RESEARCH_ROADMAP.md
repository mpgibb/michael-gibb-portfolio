# Commercial research roadmap

All four commercial studies have completed synthetic evaluations and reproducible source. They are demonstrations of analytical methods, not client achievements or deployed decision systems. Independent technical review remains pending.

1. **Marketing effectiveness:** preserve randomization, known truth, pre-period adjustment and fixed-treatment cost sensitivity. Next: independent review and an authorized real experiment specification.
2. **Revenue forecasting:** twelve rolling origins, three horizons, competing-risk baselines, calibration and stress scenarios are complete. Next: versioned real CRM snapshots, booked-revenue definitions and drift/fallback rules. Preserve the observed slowdown failure and simpler-model comparison.
3. **Customer retention/value:** matured temporal cohorts, survival/value prediction, cross-fitted policy learning and fresh randomized policy evaluation are complete. Next: a real, budget-limited randomized intervention. Do not claim profitability when the interval spans loss and gain.
4. **Operational planning:** single-site staffing, overtime recourse, exact resource constraints and a 52-week rolling decision evaluation are complete. Next: validate service floors, shortage economics and actual labor constraints before a shadow pilot.

Sports analytics and Other industries are future collections only. Adding an industry requires a defined decision, suitable data and reviewable source. Do not restore retired material to populate them. No new research outside Commercial is claimed complete in this release.

Keep protocols, seeds, lockfiles, data/source hashes, reports and source ZIP manifests synchronized. Publish every prespecified scenario, including negative results. Correctness checks and simulation do not substitute for independent scientific review. No paid services, live APIs or recurring monitoring are required.
