# Commercial research roadmap

1. **Marketing effectiveness and incrementality** is the flagship. Preserve the reproducible randomized synthetic study, uncertainty, known truth, negative robustness result and fixed-treatment cost sensitivity. Next: independent technical review, then a real experiment specification with authorized data and measurement design. Do not infer a spend-response curve from one campaign effect.
2. **Sales pipeline and revenue forecasting:** create point-in-time synthetic event histories, fix forecast horizons and temporal holdouts, compare practical stage-based baselines with conversion/timing models. Evaluate aggregate revenue, calibration and intervals.
3. **Customer retention and lifetime value:** define lifecycle and outcome windows, separate attrition prediction from treatment response, evaluate finite-horizon contribution and a randomized intervention.
4. **Operational planning under uncertainty:** choose one staffing or inventory decision, define costs and feasible resources, compare forecast-plus-policy baselines on future demand scenarios.

The final three are plans, not implemented or evaluated commercial applications. Publish repository links only when real reviewable source exists. Keep essential evidence visible, with supplementary diagnostics in expandable sections. Methods must serve a business decision. Use data with clear rights or documented synthetic generators; preserve configurations, seeds, code provenance and limitations. Never report invented career or client outcomes.

Automated correctness checks and simulation results do not replace Michael's independent technical review. No paid services, live APIs or recurring monitoring are needed for this roadmap. The commercial scope replaces all earlier research priorities; do not restore retired content from old exports, prompts or branches.
