Chicago skyline reference and artwork credit

The header is a simplified lake view over Monroe Harbor, based on the photograph dated July 2, 2022, at 41.872479, -87.605046. It is not an exact reproduction of every building or a survey of the skyline in 2026. Apparent size and left-to-right placement follow this view; foreground buildings obscure the lower part of 875 North Michigan Avenue.
